# Small-cage size transport

`malemod_base.control_transport` supplies default-normalized source mapping
samples and independent axial, radial, head and lobe factors for five controls.
It keeps the already fitted asset unchanged at UI 50. This is a small-cage
preview backend, not Wolverine authored morph tables, refined glans transport,
coupled pelvic growth, mechanical state or the eight physics controls.

Adapters assign factors using their measured native frames and named joints.
Scale a hierarchy once at its appropriate root; do not multiply the same factor
at every descendant. Original seam influences and body-part boundaries remain
authoritative. A render test of control independence and extremes is separate
from mapping and native graph acceptance.

Witcher adopts this transport first on its observed working 104-joint layer.
Wolverine retains its full authored solver; it may adopt the same mapping tests
and normalized preview contract without replacing that solver. Every future
spoke can consume this module and must record its own joint/axis/region bindings,
supported subset, native round-trip and observed gameplay independently.
