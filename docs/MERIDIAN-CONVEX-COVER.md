# Convex contact cover and spike containment

The October8 retail rejection overrides the earlier shape metric. That metric
looked only along longitudes and missed very large transverse returns. A fresh
native all-four-controls100 jump/movement replay reproduced both failures.

Separate-solid radial corrections divide by a near-tangent plane slope and
can create large spikes. Existing triangle certificates only tested sampled
primitive planes, missing separating directions of a triangle's own plane.
Failed continuity repair also modified the mesh before returning false, and
transport/repair could move the current terminal pole.

Base now owns optional exact support functions on its SDK-free convex Hull,
triangle-plane separation, a common convex cover bridging the moving solids,
scale-dependent contact correction trust regions, and transactional bounded
continuity repair with a fixed current pole. Fairing examines returns along
and across longitudes, retains incident collision/orientation checks and
projects transverse moves back into the current longitude plane. A failed
full solve can generate a fresh WalkMeridians display envelope; it never
publishes partially corrected failed geometry. That envelope is separately
certified and is not automatically a collision pass.

Wolverine supplies exact analytic link/dome/ovoid support from current measured
frames. It seeds the full walk from the common convex cover and uses a fresh
current-pose cover on failure. The posterior-body guard was disabled for the
cloth-only native isolation pass, then restored for combined motion checks.
32rays,24rows,
bindingrevision5 and fixed trim/UV lineage remain. Other spokes adopt through
a tested Base pin and their own measured primitive support mappings; Witcher
adoption is deferred.

Assertion-enabled regressions cover missed separating directions, intersecting
chords, support containment, immutable seam/pole, failed repair publication,
cached/uncached parity and changing obstacles. Private native captures/replays
are kept outside Git. Static/offline checks are not an observed gameplay pass;
continuous native movement, attachment inspection and campaign checks remain
required. Uncertified display envelopes and extreme-seam facets must be
reported separately from successful triangle certificates.

The movable interior certificate is separate from prescribed sewn-edge and
terminal-pole contact. A clear movable interior may reuse its anatomy-follow
bindings even if the fixed edge fails its whole-surface certificate. The failed
whole-surface result remains reported as uncertified; it is not relabeled clear.
This prevents an immovable contact from forcing full walking every frame.

Native checking showed that excluding the pinned faces alone was insufficient:
some display envelopes also fail movable-interior contact. Those envelopes may
follow only within a separate geometric budget measured from their original
wrap. Relative rig translation/rotation is limited to35percent of each measured
axis; every face rejects inversion and edge stretch outside2/3 to1.5 of its
original length. Common actor motion is factored out. Larger shape changes
rebuild a current-pose envelope. Uncertified reuse stays explicitly uncertified;
this bounds display deformation and does not assert collision clearance.
